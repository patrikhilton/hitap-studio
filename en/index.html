HOW IT WORKS

From idea to finished website

You don't need to know anything about web design or technology. I'll guide you through the entire process – from our first conversation to a finished and published website.

01

We talk

You tell me about your business, your customers and what you want your website to help you achieve.

02

I build

I design and develop your website based on your business, your requirements and your content.

03

You review

You'll review the website and provide feedback before it goes live. Two revision rounds are included.

04

We launch

Once everything is ready, I'll publish your website and make sure it works across mobile, tablet and desktop.
